    The verdict, and the fine print on it

    10 of 10 acceptable as astrology, none rejected, on the owner’s own chart — marriage timing and partner, career direction and change, money, health, property, education, a gemstone question and a horary number. The bar was 9. This is the precedent for every Stage B topic group: the prompt, the eleven-tool surface and the reference swap produce readings a practitioner will stand behind.

    It is bound to claude-sonnet-5 and to nothing else. Principle III (v3.0.0) says a correctness verdict does not transfer between models, so when the preview moves to a self-hosted model at launch, both this verdict and the latency figure have to be earned again. That is written next to each of them in the spec rather than left to anyone’s memory. A second caveat, smaller and real: Ghazipur’s coordinates were entered by hand (25.58°N, 83.58°E) because no geocoder is wired, so the chart is accurate to about a town’s width — fine for judging method and tone, not for anything that turns on an exact cusp boundary.

    Thirty seconds holds, with half the budget spare

    SC-008 was measured end to end through the whole guarded path, not around the model call: 10 of 10 answers inside 30 seconds, median 14.3s, slowest 15.3s, fastest 3.9s. The spread between slowest and median is one second — a system bounded by tool rounds rather than by a long tail. The harness times each question itself, so the figure is reproducible rather than a stopwatch reading, and it is printed, not asserted: this is a shared API over a home connection, and failing a build on a slow afternoon only teaches everyone to ignore a red bar. No amendment to the criterion was needed. Nothing measured before the turn bound was fixed means anything — one question had taken 24 minutes.

    Three defects that only running it could find

    The last snapshot listed five. These three are worse, because two of them had a full green test suite sitting on top of them:

    1. Spring AI does not run the tool loop, and the stub had pretended it did. The framework returns a tool-call response and leaves the round to the caller. The test stub emulated a loop the framework never provided, so 116 tests passed over code that could not have worked in production at all. The loop now lives in the answerer, which is also the only place the round cap and the deadline can be enforced rather than hoped for. A stub must be wrong in the same direction as reality or it is worse than no stub.
    2. With thinking enabled, the reply is invisible. Finish reason end_turn, 1,877 output tokens, zero readable characters: Spring AI 2.0.1 puts the content in a package-private message type and neither the text nor the tool calls surface. No public accessor exists. Thinking is off as a documented workaround with the evidence attached, not as a preference — and part of the one remaining open task is the cost of that.
    3. A decline nobody had to earn. Two answerable questions came back “I have no basis” after zero tool calls — a gemstone question, though the gems sheet is answerable at manifest row 45, and a horary number, though the mindset table needs nothing but the number. The prompt told the model to decline when “the tools give you nothing” and never said to look first, so a decline could be an opening assumption rather than a finding. It now has to be earned by a search. A false decline is as damaging as an invention and much harder to see: it looks like modesty.

    The advisory sentence: a fix, a measurement, and an accepted cost

    A marriage reading ended with “consult a qualified doctor”; an education reading ended with “please consult a doctor about any health concern”. Eight of the ten carried a health or longevity tail, mostly about something the querent never asked.

    Half of that was a real divergence from the requirement. SPEC-011 FR-014 says the most severe class used, and the code read it as the class returned — and getChartPromises with no house returns all twelve, so the 8th house’s longevity matters attached themselves to any question asked broadly. Sensitivity is now attributed per house and counts only for the houses the model asked about by number; content with no house behind it still always counts, because asking for it is the evidence the answer is about it. FR-014 was amended to define the word that had been load-bearing while undefined.

    And then the re-run came back eight of ten again. The reason is structural to KP: the Lagna is read in every judgement, and house 1 alone carries 12 longevity rules and 35 health rules — among them Strong Lagna and Long life (over 65 Yrs) for normal people. A chart that promises a long life is what triggers the caution about death. The remaining cause is how those matters are classified in the licensed workbook, not how the code reads them.

    Decided: accepted. The tail stays — conservative, and correct in the narrow sense that the content it names really was among the results. Recorded as a known, measured, accepted cost rather than an open defect, with a pointer to the import for whoever reconsiders it. The plumbing fix stands on its own; accepting the tail accepts the residue, not the bug.
